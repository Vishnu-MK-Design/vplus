Native application screenshots

The case study also shows actual V+ Windows 0.6.6 screens from an isolated profile: Home, Movies, Series, Anime, quick search, movie details, cast and anime episodes. They document the application interface and its real catalogue. Posters, title treatments, backdrops and cast photography visible inside those screenshots belong to their respective rights holders and are not V+ original artwork. Catalogue metadata and artwork are supplied through the app’s existing sources, including Cinemeta/MetaHub, TMDB, AniList and TVmaze; individual cast credits remain visible where supplied. No affiliation or endorsement is claimed.

These still application captures are separate from the openly licensed demo media credited above. The Blender film licences do not apply to commercial film or television artwork inside native screenshots. Those titles and their streams are not added to the public playable demo, and the screenshots do not verify that every title is available for playback.

What this demo includes

  • Curated film discovery, genre collections, title details and live search.
  • My List and playback position saved in this browser’s local storage.
  • The desktop control bar, keyboard shortcuts, neutral option menus and cached timeline previews.
  • Real English captions for the Tears of Steel excerpt.

What remains separate

The full Windows app’s live catalogue, TV/anime metadata, episode tracking, native window actions, local subtitle-file import and external playback providers are outside this browser demo. Ratings and popularity metrics are not fabricated for the curated films. Original-film runtime metadata is distinct from the 45-second demo playback duration.

External-provider availability and subtitle support vary. AnimeEmbed has not been integrated: its real subtitle rendering remains unverified. This demo’s working local captions do not verify AnimeEmbed or another external provider. No account, cloud sync, payment or download service is represented here.

Your data

No analytics, accounts, third-party playback frames or remote metadata requests are used in this demo. Saved titles, preferences and watch position stay in local browser storage on this origin. You can remove them through your browser’s site-data controls. Media and application files are served by the demo’s host.
